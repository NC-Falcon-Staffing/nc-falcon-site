A family business built in the field

NC Falcon was founded by the Falcon family in North Carolina, on more than 20 years of real electrical industry experience.

Our Mission

Dependable manpower. Steady opportunity.

NC Falcon provides dependable electrical manpower solutions for contractors while creating consistent work opportunities for skilled electricians.

As a family-owned business, we focus on relationships, communication, reliability, and flexibility. Our goal is simple: help contractors solve their manpower challenges while giving electricians the steady work and professional growth they deserve.

Our Story

NC Falcon was founded by the Falcon family in North Carolina and built from real experience in the electrical industry.

Ownership brings more than 20 years of electrical experience, while the next generation continues to grow the business through recruiting, contractor relationships, and workforce development.

We've placed more than 100 electricians over time and keep roughly 20 actively working. Most come to us through referrals, industry connections, and word of mouth.
